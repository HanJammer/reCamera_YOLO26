reCamera ONNX → CVI Model Converter

Upload a YOLO detection ONNX and optionally replace the bundled test image. The tool derives YOLO11/YOLO26 output names, runs TPU-MLIR conversion in Docker, and returns a reCamera-ready .cvimodel + model.json.

v1 scope: detection models only, CV181x target. INT8 is the default/recommended reCamera mode; F16 is available for experiments. Segmentation/pose/classification are not supported yet.
